# Grand Horizon Hotel - Amenities & Activities

## Hotel Facilities

### Pool & Beach

**Main Pool**
- Location: 3rd Floor
- Hours: 7:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Temperature: Heated (26°C)
- Depth: 1.2m - 2.5m
- Features: Lap lanes, hydrotherapy corner

**Kids Pool**
- Location: 3rd Floor (adjacent to main)
- Hours: 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM
- Depth: 0.5m
- Features: Water slides, shallow play area

**Private Beach**
- Location: Ground Floor (accessible via elevator)
- Hours: 6:00 AM - 7:00 PM
- Access: Towels provided at beach entrance
- Services: Sun loungers, umbrellas (free)

## Hotel Rules & Policies

### General

- **Check-in:** 3:00 PM
- **Check-out:** 11:00 AM
- **Late checkout:** $50 until 2:00 PM
- **Minimum stay:** None
- **Age requirement:** 18+ to book room

### Payments

- Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Cash
- Deposit: $100/night (authorize, not charged)
- Currency: USD (others accepted at conversion)

### Behavior

- No smoking in rooms (designated areas outside)
- Quiet hours: 10:00 PM - 7:00 AM
- No pets (service animals welcome)
- Proper attire required in lobby/restaurants

### Pool & Beach

- No diving
- No glass containers
- Children under 12 must be supervised
- No loud music/behavior

### Fitness Center

- Proper athletic attire required
- Wipe down equipment after use
- 30-minute limit on busy equipment
- No personal training without authorization

### Spa

- Arrive 15 minutes early
- Cancel 24 hours in advance
- Pregnant guests: consult physician first
- No children under 16 in treatment areas
